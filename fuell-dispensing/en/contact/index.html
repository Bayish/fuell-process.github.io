How to prepare your enquiry

The more we know about your task, the faster you get a reliable concept:

  • Components with viscosity, Ex classification and annual consumption
  • Containers and batch sizes (smallest and largest quantity)
  • Orders per day and required accuracy
  • Existing storage, space and Ex zones
  • Required connection to ERP, MES or colour measurement

Talk to us about your dispensing task

Send us your products, the number of components and the required throughput. In the pre-engineering phase we show you which system fits your production.
