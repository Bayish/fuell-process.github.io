Glossary

Sedimentation

Sedimentation: Sedimentation is the settling of solid particles, such as pigments or fillers, in a liquid.

This creates a concentration gradient in storage containers and pipes: at the top the product is too weakly pigmented, at the bottom too strongly. The dispensed quantity then no longer matches the recipe.

Countermeasures are agitators and regular recirculation. In CALIPSO, recirculation and stirring intervals are defined for this; with the FS XS, all components are recirculated via the valves.
