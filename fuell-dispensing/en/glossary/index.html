Dispensing technology glossary

38 technical terms on dispensing systems, tank farms, agitators, Ex protection and software – explained briefly and clearly.

To the guide
ABCDEGILMOPRSTUVW

A

AgitatorAn agitator is a machine that moves liquids in a container with a rotating impeller in order to homogenise them or keep solids in suspension.Aircut valveThe Aircut valve is a dispensing valve that Füll developed together with Zeller & Gmelin to dispense highly viscous substances accurately.ATEXATEX refers to the European directives on explosion protection: 2014/34/EU for equipment and protective systems, 1999/92/EC for the protection of workers.Automatic valve cleaningAutomatic valve cleaning cleans the heads of the dispensing valves mechanically so that no product residues dry on or are carried over into other containers.

B

Batch traceabilityBatch traceability means being able to prove for every batch produced which raw materials were used, in what quantity, when and by whom.

C

CALIPSOCALIPSO (Color And Liquid Processing Software) is Füll’s dispensing software, used to operate dispensing systems and manage orders, recipes, stocks and logs.Coarse and fine flow dispensingIn coarse and fine flow dispensing, most of the quantity is first dispensed with a high flow rate and then accurately completed with a low flow rate shortly before the target.

D

Dispensing accuracyDispensing accuracy indicates how close the quantity actually dispensed is to the target quantity.Dispensing centreA dispensing centre is the central part of a dispensing system, where the dispensing valves of all components are brought together and dispense into the target container.Dispensing logThe dispensing log is the automatic record of every dispensing and filling operation of a dispensing system.Dispensing valveA dispensing valve releases a raw material in a controlled quantity from the supply line into the target container.

E

ERP connectionERP connection means that a dispensing system receives orders and recipes directly from the company’s ERP system.Ex protectionEx protection (explosion protection) covers all measures that prevent explosions in areas with flammable gases, vapours or dusts.Exchangeable containersExchangeable containers are transportable containers such as buckets, pails, drums or IBCs from which material is dispensed directly and which are replaced when empty.

G

Gravimetric dispensingGravimetric dispensing means that the quantity added is measured by its mass – with a scale or a mass flow meter.

I

IBC (intermediate bulk container)An IBC is a large packaging for liquids, usually a 1,000-litre plastic container in a lattice cage on a pallet, or a metal container.IBC agitatorAn IBC agitator (also called container or traverse agitator) is a top-mounted agitator fitted on a support for stirring in IBCs.In-can tintingIn-can tinting is the tinting of paints directly in the sales container as needed, from a batch size of one container.

L

Level indicatorA level indicator shows the fill level of a container and can also serve as overfill protection.Lid agitatorA lid agitator is a top-mounted agitator that is placed on an adapted container lid and stirs directly in the delivery container.

M

Mass flow meterA mass flow meter measures the mass flowing through a pipe directly, without the target vessel having to stand on a scale.

O

Overfill protectionOverfill protection prevents a container from overflowing during filling and liquid from escaping.

P

Piston pumpA piston pump delivers a defined volume by having a piston draw in liquid and push it out again – a typical element of volumetric dispensing.PLC (programmable logic controller)A PLC is an industrial control device that controls machines and systems according to a stored program.Pneumatic driveA pneumatic drive uses compressed air to generate movement, for example for agitators, lifting devices or valves.

R

RecipeA recipe is the documented composition of a product: which components it contains and in which proportions.RecirculationRecirculation means that a raw material is regularly pumped through a ring line from storage to the dispensing valve and back.Remote maintenanceRemote maintenance means that service technicians analyse and support a system via a secure data connection without being on site.RetrofitRetrofit (also revamp) refers to modernising an existing system instead of replacing it with a new one.

S

SedimentationSedimentation is the settling of solid particles, such as pigments or fillers, in a liquid.Semi-finished productA semi-finished product is a prefabricated intermediate product, for example a pigment paste or a stock solution, from which the end product is made by mixing it with other components.Stand agitatorA stand agitator is an agitator on a stand that is lowered for stirring in drums, containers, IBCs or small containers.

T

Tank farmA tank farm is the entirety of the stationary containers in which raw materials and semi-finished products are stored for production.Tinting pasteA tinting paste is a highly concentrated, pigmented product used to adjust a base paint to the desired shade.

U

UV-curing inks and coatingsUV-curing inks and coatings do not cure by drying but by polymerisation under ultraviolet radiation.

V

ViscosityViscosity is the measure of how thick a substance is, i.e. its internal resistance to flowing.Volumetric dispensingVolumetric dispensing measures the quantity added by its volume instead of its mass.

W

WHG specialist companyA WHG specialist company is a company that, under German water law, may install, maintain and repair systems for handling substances hazardous to water.
