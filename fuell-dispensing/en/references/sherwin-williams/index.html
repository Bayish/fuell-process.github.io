Case study

Sherwin-Williams: automated production with large tank farm

What began at the European Coatings Show in Nuremberg with an enquiry for a few drum systems became an extensive plant project: Füll developed the process equipment for the Sherwin-Williams plant in El Salvador and delivered it turnkey.

  • 64components in dispensing system and tank farm
  • 9further dispensing systems connected
  • 25–50 ttanks in the large tank farm
  • ≈ 18 monthsproject duration from March 2011

The customer

The Sherwin-Williams Corporation, headquartered in Cleveland (Ohio), manufactures paints, coatings and building materials and sells them in around 4,000 of its own paint and coatings stores. Its brands include OECE, Sayerlack, ARTI and Lineablu.

The task

Originally, the project was about individual adaptations of a Füll drum system. After initial talks and a one-week visit to Central America, it was clear: extensive construction, modernisation and automation measures were needed for the production of paints and coatings. The goals:

  • Reproducible quality of the solutions and coatings
  • Significantly improved safety and explosion protection to international standards, liquid semi-finished products in closed tank systems
  • Integration of the systems into the existing ERP system
  • Cleanliness and therefore more safety in production
  • Automation of the main production areas

Solution: custom engineering based on proven Füll systems

After a thorough on-site analysis, the process technology and processes were developed, produced and programmed. The project started in March 2011 and took about 18 months; parts of the building had to be newly built.

  • 1 automatic 64-component dispensing system with a complete 64-component tank farm for colour pastes, additives and solvents
  • 1 fully automatic system for producing solvents for the entire range of the coatings industry
  • 9 further dispensing systems for solid and liquid auxiliaries, connected to the central Füll control with explosion-proof terminals
  • Basic and detail engineering for a customer-owned large tank farm with 25 to 50 tonne tanks
  • Connection of all systems to the central computer: orders are loaded centrally to all terminals, every step is confirmed by password

All systems and equipment are built according to ATEX – essential, because some solvents become critical at temperatures that are reached every day in El Salvador.

Result from the customer's point of view

Less manual work

Fully automatic operation: only a few simple manual steps, fewer errors and health risks.

Batch reproducibility

Every batch matches the previous one – customers can buy exactly the same shade again even years later.

Operator safety

Closed systems; orders and formulations come online from the ERP.

Operational safety

All plant components designed for Ex areas and ATEX-compliant.

Cleanliness

Closed systems reduce cleaning costs and increase operational safety.

Investment

From the customer's point of view, the investment has paid off: maximum efficiency, cost reduction and investment security.

“The Füll team trained our technical staff excellently and supports us around the clock in emergencies.”

Juan Francisco Sifontes, President & COO
