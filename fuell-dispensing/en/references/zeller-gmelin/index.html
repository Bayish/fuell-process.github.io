Case study

Zeller & Gmelin: process optimisation of printing ink systems

Production plant with storage system for raw materials and semi-finished products for conventional and UV-curing offset and flexo printing inks – redesigned from goods receipt to filling.

  • 38 °Cconstantly heated, UV-protected storage
  • up to 100dispensing valves, expandable
  • 2013project start
  • 2016award from the state of Baden-Württemberg

The customer

Zeller & Gmelin, a company with a long tradition from Eislingen near Stuttgart, develops and produces lubricants, industrial chemicals and printing inks and operates worldwide with 16 subsidiaries. It is one of the leading international suppliers of radiation-curing printing inks and varnishes – for cardboard, plastic and metal packaging as well as labels.

The task

At the end of 2012, the management decided to redesign the production area for radiation-curing printing ink systems – to reduce production costs, optimise processes and secure growth. Further goals: better ergonomics, leaner processes, optimised internal logistics.

After a Europe-wide search, three specialised companies were shortlisted. Füll was chosen for its product expertise and the planning know-how to develop and build a suitable system and integrate it optimally into the processes – especially for the gentle handling and highly accurate dispensing of reactive, UV-curing raw materials.

Solution: from heated storage to the aircut valve

At the beginning of 2013, the project started with a 3D model of the entire plant – tank system, heated storage, pipework system and dispensing. This allowed everyone involved to identify critical points early on. For the heating chamber, electrics, tank system and pipework, Füll worked with specialised partners.

  • Storage for liquid components at a constant 38 °C so that the materials remain pumpable
  • No UV light in the storage area to avoid unwanted polymerisation
  • Newly developed aircut valve from several weeks of joint trials for the high-viscosity substances
  • System expandable to up to 100 dispensing valves

Result

Resource efficiency

After about 2.5 years of operation, the state of Baden-Württemberg recognised the efficient, resource-saving use of raw materials in 2016.

Ergonomics and safety

Instead of transporting 200-litre containers, pumps convey the ink through pipework – transport accidents are a thing of the past.

Purchasing and output

Delivery by tanker; thanks to the reliable availability of raw materials at the plant, output increased sustainably.

Quality

Automation makes the processes safer and more reproducible; the goal of cost savings was also fully achieved.

“We are very satisfied with the after sales service. Many things can be clarified directly via a remote maintenance system between service and production.”

Andreas Beutinger, plant manager at the printing ink plant
