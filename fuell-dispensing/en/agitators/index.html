Why agitators are crucial in storage

In most dispensing systems, agitators are a central part of the storage technology. Together with pipework designed for circulation, they ensure homogeneous raw materials – and therefore high quality in dispensing.

With correct process design of the storage system, subsequent corrections such as re-tinting are significantly reduced.

The types

Built-in agitators

For mounting from above on an agitator nozzle or flange. Used for homogenising, circulating and keeping in suspension in tanks and storage containers.

FS 245

Lid agitators

Top-mounted agitators for pails, drums and metal containers – the container lid is adapted. The FS 245 series is designed for 25 L pails and 200 L drums; stirring takes place directly in the delivery container.

IBC agitators

Also called container or cross-beam agitators: mounted on a support above the IBC, usually with a folding impeller for the small opening of 1,000 L plastic IBCs.

FS 240 · FS 241 · FS 242

Stand agitators

For stirring in drums, containers, IBCs or small containers – free-standing, wall-mounted, floor/wall or mobile, each designed for the container and type of installation. Lifting and lowering is usually pneumatic, electric is also possible. The FS 241 e Ex is also available with roller conveyor.

Special agitators

When no standard agitator fits: from adapting a base agitator to a new design for a special container to fully automatic process integration with an automatic cleaning system.

Frequently asked questions

Does Füll also supply agitators without a dispensing system?

Yes. Agitators are available for tanks, containers and delivery containers; Füll sales helps with the selection.

Which agitator fits a 1,000 L IBC?

Usually an IBC agitator with folding impeller or a stand agitator – depending on product, viscosity and how often the container is changed.
