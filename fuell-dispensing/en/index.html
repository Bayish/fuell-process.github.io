Where your production loses time and material today

Many plants still weigh, decant and transport by hand. That costs time, creates variation between batches and increases the risk in Ex areas.

Weighing by hand

Dozens of components per formulation, each one weighed separately. That time is missing for the real work on formulations and quality.

Moving drums

Carrying raw materials in 200-litre drums from storage to production takes effort and carries the risk of transport accidents.

Open containers in Ex areas

Decanting solvent-based products in open containers endangers people and plant and contaminates production.

Gaps in documentation

Without a dispensing log it is hard to prove how a batch was made. Re-ordering the identical colour shade becomes a risk.

An automatic dispensing system solves these points together: closed systems, controlled dispensing and every batch documented.

From goods receipt to the finished mix

Füll plans not only the dispensing system but the whole material flow: from storage through the pipework to filling. Everything is controlled by CALIPSO, the dispensing software Füll has developed since 1993.

  1. 1

    Store

    Raw materials and intermediates in container, tank or heated storage, as closed systems.

  2. 2

    Convey

    Pumps and pipework bring the components straight to the dispensing system, without moving drums.

  3. 3

    Circulate

    Circulation and stirring intervals keep products homogeneous and prevent sedimentation.

  4. 4

    Dispense

    Gravimetrically with scales or mass flow meters, or volumetrically, with an accuracy down to 0.1 g.

  5. 5

    Stir and fill

    Dispensing goes straight into the container, from small cans to large containers. Füll agitators handle the mixing.

  6. 6

    Document

    Every dispensing and filling operation is stored for quality assurance and batch traceability, optionally linked to ERP or MES.

Planning, manufacturing and assembly take place in Idstein. In-house software, PLC and electronics departments keep control systems and interfaces close at hand.

How Zeller & Gmelin dispenses UV printing inks automatically

Zeller & Gmelin in Eislingen, Germany, is one of the leading suppliers of radiation-curing printing inks. From 2013, Füll redesigned the production of these inks: from goods receipt through storage heated to 38 °C and protected from UV light, and the pipework, to dispensing.

For the highly viscous, reactive raw materials, both teams developed a new Aircut valve in several weeks of joint trials. The system can be extended to up to 100 dispensing valves. In 2016 the state of Baden-Württemberg recognised the resource-efficient use of raw materials.

Instead of transporting 200-litre drums, pumps now convey the ink through pipework to the system.

Many things can be clarified directly between service and production via a remote maintenance system.

Frequently asked questions

Which products can Füll dispensing systems handle?

Low- to high-viscosity liquids and pastes: water- and solvent-based components and UV-curing products, for example paints, coatings, printing inks, additives, resins, adhesives and silicones.

Do you dispense gravimetrically or volumetrically?

Both. Gravimetric systems measure with scales or mass flow meters; volumetric systems work, for example, with the HX5 dispensing unit or the VX5. The right choice depends on reproducibility, container sizes and throughput.

Are the systems suitable for Ex areas?

Yes. Füll is ATEX-certified for manufacturing equipment. Systems, agitators and terminals are designed for potentially explosive atmospheres.

Can the system be connected to ERP or MES?

Yes. Our own software and PLC departments connect the systems to higher-level systems. At Sherwin-Williams, all orders are loaded centrally from the ERP system to the Füll terminals.

Can existing systems be extended or modernised?

Yes. Thanks to the modular design, additional valves, pipes and storage tanks can be integrated. In a retrofit we renew controls, valves or complete assemblies, also on other manufacturers' systems.
