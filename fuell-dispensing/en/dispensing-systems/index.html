Dispensing systems: flexible, fast, precise

Our core business is the custom design and construction of dispensing systems to the required process parameters. Applications differ widely even within one industry – which is why every system becomes a tailor-made one-off after careful analysis.

Gravimetric dispensing centres

Gravimetric dispensing measures mass – independent of density and temperature. The dispensing centres come in different sizes up to 64 components, for different valve types and volumes, with valves in one or two rows. A servo motor moves the valve plate; the valve stroke is driven with high precision by a servo drive.

Compact

FS XS

The smallest fully automatic dispensing centre by Füll, developed as a cost-effective alternative for very small to medium printing ink consumption – without compromising on quality and traceability.

  • valves arranged in a fixed circle
  • for Ex and non-Ex environments
  • water-based, solvent-based and UV-curing products
  • all components are recirculated through the valves
  • compact and modular – ideal where space is tight
  • monitored via PLC and CALIPSO
Smallest containers

FS MS

For dispensing into very small container openings. The valves sit in one row; the scale moves with the container between the valves.

  • valves in one row
  • bottles with openings > 20 mm
  • container holders for different containers
  • small throughputs
Production

FS 2005.2

Dispensing centre for manufacturers of paints, additives and printing inks as well as packaging producers – from high annual throughputs to dispensing into very small containers such as cans.

  • up to 32 valves in two rows
  • buckets, 25 kg pails
  • scales or HX5 volumetric dispensing unit
  • servo-controlled valves
  • water-based, solvent-based and UV-curing products
High throughput

FS 182.2

The large series for many components and large containers. The field of application depends on the number, throughput and physical properties of the components.

  • more than 32 valves in two rows
  • pails, 200 L drums, process vessels
  • medium to high throughputs
  • scales or HX5
  • servo-controlled valves
Process feeding

FS 188

Developed for discontinuous and continuous feeding processes – wherever reactive components are dispensed and possibly mixed in parallel to supply downstream processes such as laminating machines, dip tanks or curtain coaters.

  • gravimetric measurement
  • scales or mass flow meters
  • high reproducibility and traceability

Laboratory and development: LX5

You develop, optimise and correct formulations, keep dozens of base components in stock for this and do it all by hand? Then you can leave the weighing to the gravimetric LX5 laboratory dispensing system – freeing up capacity to evaluate your dispensing series.

  • Highest demands on dispensing accuracy and reproducible results
  • For series formulations in the paint and coatings industry (since 2009)
  • Integrates seamlessly into laboratory and production processes – you decide the level of automation

Volumetric dispensing and tinting

Volumetric systems dispense several components at the same time – ideal for tinting into pre-filled containers.

Volumetric

VX5

Compact volumetric dispensing system with patented double-action piston pump for the semi-industrial wholesale sector – in different sizes and modules, also for UV components.

  • storage canisters with integrated stirrer (5 or 10 L)
  • dispensing also into small container openings
  • simultaneously volumetric or gravimetrically controlled one after the other
  • with internal canisters, external tanks or combined with gravimetric systems
  • options: colour measurement interfaces, electrically height-adjustable container table, container carousel
Label printing

U-VX5

Compact dispensing system for UV, solvent-based and water-based printing inks of low to medium viscosity – an affordable option for small to medium consumption. Values marked * depend on rheology and configuration.

  • up to 24 components
  • stainless steel tanks of 5, 10 or 25 L, with agitator and recirculation
  • volumetric or gravimetrically controlled (3.6 kg scale, resolution 0.01 g)
  • dispensing quantities up to 5 kg, smallest quantity 0.05 ml*
  • dispensing accuracy ± 1 %*, pump capacity approx. 1.2 L/min*
  • 5 kg in under 3 min volumetrically, approx. 4–5 min gravimetrically controlled*
Drum supply

FS-SD drum dispenser

Single dispenser for extracting printing inks directly from 200 kg drums.

  • for UV ink systems of low to medium viscosity, e.g. pre-print white or print varnishes
  • conveyed by a compressed-air diaphragm pump
  • freely programmable recirculation for homogenisation
Batch size 1

In-can tinting

On-demand production in the sales container: place the container with barcode, release – the base is pre-dispensed gravimetrically, tinted simultaneously and volumetrically with universal pastes and stirred in the self-cleaning agitator.

  • different containers via universal container holders
  • freely selectable level of automation
  • on-demand production from batch size 1

Why simultaneous dispensing saves time

VX5 technology consists of a double piston pump, pump selector, tank with recirculation and agitator, and a simultaneous dispensing head. Because all formulation components are dispensed at the same time, the duration depends on the largest component – not on the sum of all components.

Calculation example from the Füll presentation: a 3 kg batch of four components, average pump capacity 1.5 L/min:

ComponentShareQuantityDispensing time
A46 %1.38 kg45 s
B17 %0.51 kg19 s
C33 %0.99 kg29 s
D4 %0.12 kg5 s

Dispensed simultaneously, the batch takes about 45 seconds – dispensed one after the other, it would take about 98 seconds.

What all Füll systems have in common

  • Custom-built: valves are individually adapted to flow behaviour, density and viscosity.
  • Homogeneous raw materials: recirculation and agitators prevent sedimentation.
  • In-house software: CALIPSO manages orders, recipes, stock levels and logs.
  • Connectivity: interfaces to ERP, MES and colour measurement.
  • Safety: design for potentially explosive atmospheres according to ATEX.
  • Modular: valves, pipes and storage tanks can be added later.

Frequently asked questions

How accurately do Füll systems dispense?

Depending on the version, down to 0.1 g. The achievable accuracy depends on scale, valve, target quantity and product.

Are there systems for Ex areas?

Yes. The FS XS is suitable for Ex and non-Ex environments; larger systems are designed project-specifically according to ATEX.

Can I combine gravimetric and volumetric dispensing?

Yes. The FS 2005.2 and FS 182.2 can be equipped with scales or the HX5 volumetric dispensing unit; in in-can tinting both principles work together.
