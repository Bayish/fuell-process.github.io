Dispensing software for every application

The software decides how well a dispensing system fits into your processes. Füll develops it in-house: CALIPSO has controlled Füll dispensing systems since 1993 and manages all data for storing, dispensing and handling raw materials and finished mixtures.

The programs at a glance

Gravimetric

CALIPSO

For gravimetric dispensing systems, with function modules for paints and coatings as well as printing and packaging. CALIPSO (Color And Liquid Processing Software) is the standard on Füll systems.

Production

CALIPSO BATCH

For gravimetric dispensing systems in the production of paints and coatings from semi-finished products. Developed in 2012 for applications in the production environment.

Volumetric

AURORA

Recipe management program for volumetric dispensing systems, usually used in label printing. AURORA is the basis for tailor-made ERP interfaces.

VX5 / U-VX5

Dispenser Manager

Operating software of the volumetric dispensers: operation via PC with monitor, scale interface and optional online remote maintenance.

Entry level

AURORA-Mix

Specially for manual weighing: together with a scale, the system guides the operator through the process steps to the finished mixture – the cost-effective entry into dispensing technology.

Dispenser Manager: open to your systems

The Dispenser Manager is the operating system of the VX5 and U-VX5 volumetric dispensers. It is operated on a PC with monitor and can be flexibly connected to your existing systems:

  • Recipe management, e.g. AURORA
  • Colour measurement systems, e.g. X-Rite, Gretag or Datacolor
  • ERP systems, e.g. DIBAC
  • Scale interface
  • Online remote maintenance (optional)

Standard functions of CALIPSO

Order management

Create orders and assign recipes.

Recipe management

Any number of recipes with all components and proportions; the quantities to be dispensed in kg are calculated.

Recirculation and stirring intervals

For product homogeneity and against sedimentation; components are divided into groups.

Material stock

Contents of the storage tanks as a bar chart – to detect shortages and estimate consumption.

Parameter and scale management

Optimise dispensing speed and accuracy; manage several dispensing centres with several scales.

Container management

Filling volume, tare weights and tolerances of every container; plausibility check as overfill protection.

Password system

Access rights for operators at the plant and in the PC program.

Dispensing and error log

Every dispensing and filling operation is saved – sortable, filterable and exportable for batch traceability.

Optional modules

Not included in the standard scope of delivery – depending on the industry, Füll sales selects the right extensions with you.

ERP interface

Orders and recipes from your ERP system to CALIPSO via Ethernet. Every interface is adapted to the customer.

Motif management

Manage recipes for a print motif and calculate the required ink quantities from the area to be printed.

Dispensing preparation

Specify dispensing jobs and their sequence from the workstation PC – fewer entries for the operator on site.

Residual ink storage

Store residual inks from production with quantity and recipe, print a label and check for new dispensing jobs whether they can be reused.

Base storage

Virtual storage for raw materials that are not dispensed by the system, including manual components.

VOC balance

Store VOC shares per component, show the VOC quantity in the dispensing log and evaluate it over periods of time.

Colour measurement interface

Import recipe data from different colour measurement systems; the import function is adapted on customer request.

Frequently asked questions

Does CALIPSO also run on older Füll systems?

Control and software can be modernised as part of a retrofit. Füll service will clarify with you which version fits.

Can CALIPSO be connected to our ERP?

Yes, as an optional module. Every ERP interface is adapted to the customer, and the required functions are agreed together in advance.

Talk to us about your dispensing task

Send us your products, the number of components and the required throughput. In the pre-engineering phase we show you which system fits your production.
