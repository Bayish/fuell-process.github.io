How it all began: the first hand-lever valve

Werner Füll started with this hand-lever valve. In 1965 he founded the company as a fittings turning shop in Niedernhausen near Frankfurt.

In 1973 the order volume for tank farms and dispensing systems grew, and in 1977 the first automatic dispensing system followed. Füll still makes its dispensing valves itself today – now in stainless steel, with outlet sizes from 8 to 65 mm and servo-controlled.

More about our history: History of Füll Systembau.

Automatic dispensing valves

In every Füll dispensing and colour mixing system, the valves are individually adapted to the substances to be dispensed – depending on flow behaviour, density and viscosity.

  • Outlet sizes from 8 to 65 mm inner diameter
  • Flow rates up to 5.97 m³/h with a 65 mm valve
  • With needle for ultra-fine dispensing
  • Controlled pneumatically or by servo
  • Recirculation connections as standard
  • Made of stainless steel

Automatic dispensing valves in use

Manual dispensing valves

Füll's manual dispensing valves are very precise. You can dispense with these valves even during a power failure – provided they are installed with a free outlet.

  • Outlet sizes from 8 to 65 mm inner diameter
  • Flow rates up to 5.97 m³/h with a 65 mm valve
  • Made of stainless steel

Manual dispensing valve versions

Valves in the dispensing centres

A servo motor moves the valve plate, and the valve stroke is performed with high precision by servo drive. How the valves are arranged depends on the series.

SeriesValve arrangement
FS MSone row; the scale moves with the container between the valves
FS XSfixed in a circle; all components are recirculated via the valves
FS 2005.2up to 32 valves in two rows, servo-controlled
FS 182.2more than 32 valves in two rows, up to 64 components, servo-controlled

Thanks to the modular design, additional valves can be integrated into existing Füll systems. Overview: all dispensing systems.

Modernising valves: retrofit

The valve assemblies of older Füll systems can also be modernised:

AssemblyModernisation
Valve controlservo drives (FS 182.2, FS 2005.2); CALIPSO determines and optimises dispensing parameters automatically
Valve liftthe most heavily loaded assembly – fast and precise for coarse and fine flow
Valve carriageservo drive, usually 250 mm/s; the position is retained after a power failure
Valve cleaningretrofit automatic valve cleaning with rotating brushes

Frequently asked questions

Can Füll valves be retrofitted?

Thanks to the modular design, additional valves can be integrated into existing Füll systems.

Can Füll valves dispense during a power failure?

Yes, with the manual dispensing valves: with a free outlet, they can even dispense during a power failure.
