Choosing the right agitator

Agitators ensure that raw materials remain homogeneous and that solids are kept in suspension. For dispensing systems they are a central part of storage technology – and depending on the container, a different type is suitable.

Why an agitator in storage?

Many raw materials of the paint, coatings and chemical industries do not stay homogeneous by themselves: pigments settle (sedimentation), dispersions separate. In combination with a recirculation line, an agitator ensures that every dispensed quantity has the same composition. This reduces later corrections such as re-tinting.

The types at a glance

TypeMountingUse
Built-in agitatorfixed from above on a nozzle or flangetanks and storage containers: homogenising, recirculating, keeping in suspension
Lid agitatorplaced on an adapted container lidpails, drums, metal containers – stirring directly in the delivery container
IBC agitatoron a support above the IBC1,000 L plastic IBCs, usually with a folding impeller
Stand agitatorfree-standing, wall, floor/wall or mobiledrums, containers, IBCs, small containers; lifting and lowering mostly pneumatic
Special agitatorcustomer-specificapplications for which no standard type fits

Electric or pneumatic?

Agitator drives are available as electric and pneumatic versions. Pneumatic drives are widespread in potentially explosive areas because they work without an electrical ignition source at the drive. Electric drives can be controlled more finely and do not need compressed air. In the Ex area, both must be designed according to ATEX.

Further features at Füll: anti-reach-through guard, automatic lifting device and a mobile version on castors.

Selection criteria

  • Container: delivery container (lid, IBC or stand agitator) or stationary tank (built-in agitator)?
  • Opening: plastic IBCs have small openings, hence folding impellers.
  • Viscosity and solids content of the product.
  • Ex zone and hazardous substance classification.
  • Frequency of change: if the agitator is moved often, top-mounted or stand solutions are more practical.

Frequently asked questions

Can I stir directly in the delivery container?

Yes, with a lid agitator, IBC agitator or stand agitator. This makes sense when there is no storage tank or a circulation line is not sufficient.

Do I need a pneumatic agitator in the Ex area?

Not necessarily. What matters is an ATEX-compliant design for the respective zone; pneumatic drives are common there, though.
