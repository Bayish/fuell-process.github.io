Modernising a dispensing system: retrofit instead of a new build

Füll Systembau · As of November 2026 · approx. 3 min read

A dispensing system is built for many years – a service life of 20 years is nothing unusual for Füll systems. Often it is only individual assemblies that need refreshing after a long service life. A retrofit is then a cost-effective alternative to a new build.

When does a retrofit make sense?

  • The control system or computer is outdated and spare parts are becoming scarce.
  • Production or the product range has grown, and the system needs more valves or storage containers.
  • The dispensing speed or accuracy is no longer sufficient.
  • New requirements for documentation or ERP connection.
  • Wear on heavily loaded assemblies.

As with new systems, system safety has top priority. Füll defines the degree of modernisation together with the customer – and also offers retrofits for systems from other manufacturers.

Electrical control system

The computer stores system parameters, recipes, orders and logs. An industrial computer also ages and should be replaced after some time. Current control concepts communicate via Ethernet. This makes remote maintenance possible via secure VPN connections: service technicians can analyse the system down to the level of the PLC.

Valve control and valve lift

In the FS 182.2 and FS 2005.2 series, the valves are controlled by servo drives. This enables a dynamic, precise dispensing sequence; dispensing parameters are determined and optimised automatically by CALIPSO. After a power failure, no initialisation is needed.

The valve lift is the most heavily loaded assembly of a dispensing centre, because it opens and closes all valves. For coarse and fine flow dispensing it has to work quickly and precisely.

Valve carriage

In most gravimetric systems, the valves sit on a carriage that moves the active valve under the lift by servo drive – usually at 250 mm/s. The same servo motors for lift and carriage simplify spare parts stocking, and the drive knows its position after a power failure.

Automatic valve cleaning

If manual cleaning of the valve heads does not always succeed during production peaks, automatic valve cleaning is worthwhile. Rotating brushes clean the valve heads with a suitable cleaning agent that is circulated. This protects against contamination and carry-over of residues and saves operating time.

Extend instead of replace

More products, higher capacity, new raw materials: thanks to the modular design of Füll systems, additional valves, pipes, hoses and storage containers can be integrated. At Zeller & Gmelin, for example, the system can be extended to up to 100 dispensing valves.

Frequently asked questions

Does Füll also modernise systems from other manufacturers?

Yes, retrofit solutions are also available for products from other manufacturers.

How does a retrofit work?

First the system is analysed. Then Füll proposes measures – from a control upgrade to replacing complete assemblies and storage tanks. You decide on the scope.
