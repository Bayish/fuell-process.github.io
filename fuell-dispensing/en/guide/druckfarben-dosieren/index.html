Dispensing printing inks automatically

Hardly any industry has as many shades, binder systems and printing processes as printing and packaging. Providing the inks is correspondingly demanding – especially with UV-curing printing inks.

The challenge

Flexo, gravure, offset, screen and pad printing, plus flexible packaging, labels, corrugated board and decor: every combination requires its own inks. On top of that come laminating adhesives, solvents and additives. Anyone who mixes special shades by hand loses time, material and reproducibility.

Which system for which consumption?

RequirementSolution at Füll

Printing inks of low to medium viscosity, batches of 1–5 kgU-VX5 – even 100 g batches reproducibly
Medium to high annual throughput, also very small containersFS 2005.2 and FS 182.2

These systems are suitable for water-based and solvent-based as well as for UV-curing products. With the FS XS, all components are recirculated via the valves to prevent sedimentation.

Special case: UV-curing printing inks

UV-curing inks and coatings contain reactive components that polymerise under UV radiation. For dispensing this means:

  • Raw materials must not be exposed to UV light in storage or in the pipes.
  • Many components are pasty and highly viscous; they often have to be temperature-controlled so that they remain pumpable.
  • Valves must dispense highly viscous substances precisely without product dripping afterwards.

Practical example: Zeller & Gmelin

Zeller & Gmelin in Eislingen is one of the leading suppliers of radiation-curing printing inks. From 2013, Füll redesigned the production of these inks – from goods receipt via a UV-protected store heated to 38 °C and the piping system to dispensing. Planning began with a 3D model of the entire system.

In several weeks of joint trials, both teams developed a new Aircut valve for the highly viscous, reactive substances. The system can be extended to up to 100 dispensing valves. In 2016, the state of Baden-Württemberg recognised the resource-saving handling of the raw materials. Instead of transporting 200-litre containers, pumps now convey the ink to the system. Software for the printing company

The dispensing software CALIPSO contains function modules for printing and packaging. In the dispensing log, operations can be sorted for example by order, motif/design, recipe or product. For volumetric systems in label printing there is the recipe management program AURORA, and for manual weighing AURORA-Mix as a cost-effective entry into dispensing technology.

Frequently asked questions

Is a system worthwhile for smaller printing companies?

The FS XS was developed as a cost-effective alternative for very small to medium printing ink consumption, without compromising on quality and traceability.

Can I start without a dispensing system?

Yes. AURORA-Mix guides the operator through manual weighing together with a scale and is the cost-effective entry into dispensing technology.
