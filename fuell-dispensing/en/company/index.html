Company

Füll Systembau: dispensing technology since 1965

For more than 50 years, Füll Systembau GmbH has specialised in building dispensing, tank and mixing systems for low to high viscosity liquids from various industries. More than 2,500 custom dispensing and tank systems have been planned, installed and commissioned worldwide – more than half of them abroad.

What makes us different

Reliable, low-maintenance technology and close contact with our customers make Füll a reliable and innovative partner. Based on your requirements, we develop the technically and economically best solution – taking into account all legal requirements (e.g. WHG and ATEX), the dispensing principle and the spatial conditions.

Our capabilities show especially in projects with high demands on products, processes and environment. The standardised components reflect the state of the art and are the basis for cost-optimised, custom concepts.

The Idstein site is responsible for production, installation and commissioning, after sales and the sale of custom dispensing systems. The entire product range is planned, manufactured and assembled here. In-house software, PLC and electronics departments provide flexibility in plant control and in connecting to ERP or MES systems.

History

  1. 1965

    Werner Füll founds the company as a fittings turning shop in Niedernhausen near Frankfurt.

  2. 1973

    Move to larger business premises – growing order volume for tank farms and dispensing systems.

  3. 1977

    Installation of the first automatic dispensing system.

  4. 1985

    First system with IT-supported workflow (MS-DOS) and infinitely variable needle valve for drop dispensing.

  5. 1988

    Move into the company's own office and production building in Idstein.

  6. 1993

    Launch of the CALIPSO dispensing software.

  7. 1997

    Certification according to DIN EN ISO 9001.

  8. 1999

    Acquisition by the Swiss holding company Perrot Duval.

  9. 2003

    Manufacturing approval for agitators and level indicators according to ATEX.

  10. 2004

    Servo motors for valve activation and positioning.

  11. 2006

    FS XS compact automatic dispensing system.

  12. 2008

    Foundation of Füll Engineering B.V. in the Netherlands for volumetric dispensing systems.

  13. 2009

    LX5 automatic laboratory dispensing system.

  14. 2010

    VX5 volumetric dispensing system.

  15. 2012

    CALIPSO BATCH dispensing software.

  16. 2013

    Special industrial valves for high-viscosity resins and adhesives.

  17. 2014

    Network of sales agents for agitator technology in Germany.

  18. 2015

    50th company anniversary.

  19. 2016

    Tecos Bruhin AG joins the Füll Process Group.

Certifications

ISO 9001

Consulting, planning, project engineering and construction of computer-controlled precision dispensing and storage systems as well as process plants for the chemical industry. Monitored by TÜV SÜD Management Service.

ATEX

EC certificate according to Annex IV of Directive 2014/34/EU for the manufacture of various equipment. Monitored by TÜV SÜD Product Service (notified body 0123).

Certified WHG company

Installation, maintenance and repair of systems for liquids hazardous to water, especially safety-relevant parts such as overfill protection devices. Monitored by TÜV Technische Überwachung Hessen.

Certified welders

Welders with test certificate according to ISO 9606-1.

Part of the Füll Process Group

Füll Systembau belongs to the Füll Process Group, headquartered in Weinfelden (Switzerland). The group also includes Tecos Bruhin (dispensing systems for pasty products and cleaning systems), Füll Lab Automation (laboratory automation for R&D and quality control) and POLYSTONE Chemical.
