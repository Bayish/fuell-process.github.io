01About the group

One group. Four specialists – from dosing technology to cosmetics.

Füll Process S.A., based in Altnau in the canton of Thurgau, is the parent company of the Füll Process Group.

It brings together four specialised companies: Füll Systembau GmbH in Idstein (Germany), Tecos Bruhin AG in Altnau (Switzerland), Füll Lab Automation GmbH in Ostfildern (Germany) and POLYSTONE Chemical GmbH with production in Blieskastel (Germany) and Bitche (France). They develop and manufacture gravimetric and volumetric dosing systems, agitators, storage systems and laboratory automation as well as high-quality colour and beauty products for professional beauty brands worldwide.

Solutions for diverse markets: paints and coatings, printing inks, sealants and adhesives, cosmetics and care products, food, flavors, fine chemicals and textiles, and plant protection.

06FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about the group and our companies.

Which companies belong to the Füll Process Group?

The Füll Process Group comprises four companies: Füll Systembau GmbH in Idstein (Germany) for dosing systems, agitators and storage systems, Tecos Bruhin AG in Altnau (Switzerland) for gravimetric dosing systems for pasty media, Füll Lab Automation GmbH in Ostfildern (Germany) for laboratory automation and POLYSTONE Chemical GmbH with sites in Blieskastel (Germany) and Bitche (France) for nail cosmetics and permanent make-up. The parent company is Füll Process S.A. in Altnau, Switzerland.

Which industries does the Füll Process Group serve?

Paints and coatings, printing inks, sealants and adhesives, cosmetics and personal care, food, flavours, fine chemicals and textiles, and crop protection. With POLYSTONE, the group is also a private-label partner for nail cosmetics and permanent make-up.

Who should I contact with a specific enquiry?

For product enquiries, quotations and service, please contact the relevant company directly: Füll Systembau for dosing systems, agitators and storage systems, Tecos Bruhin for dosing pasty media, Füll Lab Automation for laboratory automation and POLYSTONE for cosmetics and private label. General enquiries about the group go to Füll Process S.A. in Altnau.

Where is the Füll Process Group based?

Füll Process S.A. is located at Moosgrabenstrasse 12, 8595 Altnau, in the canton of Thurgau, Switzerland. The group companies operate in Switzerland (Altnau), Germany (Idstein, Ostfildern, Blieskastel) and France (Bitche).
