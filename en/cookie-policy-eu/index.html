Cookie Policy (EU)

Last updated: November 2026 · applies to visitors from the European Economic Area and Switzerland

1. Introduction

This cookie policy explains which cookies and similar technologies the website fuell-process.com uses. More information is available in our privacy policy.

2. What are cookies and local storage?

Cookies are small text files that are stored in your browser when you visit a website. Local storage (“localStorage”) is a similar technique that stores data in the browser without sending it to the server on every page request.

3. What this website stores

This website does not set any cookies. Only a single entry is saved in your browser's local storage:

NameCategoryPurposeRetention
fpg-consentFunctionalStores your choice in the consent notice and the date, so that the notice does not appear on every page.until you delete it in your browser

This entry is required for the website to work and is not sent to us or to third parties.

4. Statistics and marketing

We currently do not use any statistics, marketing or tracking services and do not embed third-party content. If this changes, such services will only be loaded after your consent in the respective category, and this policy will be updated.

5. Managing your consent

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via “Manage consent” at the bottom of every page. You can also delete the entry in your browser settings (clear site data or cookies and site data); the notice will then appear again on your next visit.
